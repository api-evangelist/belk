# Belk

> Belk is a privately held American department-store chain headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, selling apparel, beauty, jewelry and home goods across the southeastern United States through stores and belk.com. Belk publishes NO public API, no developer portal, no OpenAPI, no SDK and no MCP server. Its only published integration surface is a mandatory X12 EDI (version 4030) supplier program routed through a VAN, documented as PDF implementation guides on the Vendor Resources page.

Generated by API Evangelist on 2026-09-19 from the belk.com Policies & Guidelines pages and the EDI Vendor FAQ (method: generated). Nothing here is a Belk-published llms.txt; www.belk.com/llms.txt answers with a PerimeterX challenge (403).

## What exists for integrators

- [Vendor Resources](https://www.belk.com/customer-service/policies-guidelines/vendor-resources/): index of the X12 4030 EDI implementation guides (212, 214, 810, 820, 824, 850, 852, 855, 856, 860), the EDI Vendor FAQ, drop-ship participant policies, transportation/TMS guides and vendor business requirements. All PDF.
- [EDI Vendor FAQ (PDF)](https://www.belk.com/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-Belk-Library/default/dwd0ec369d/media/pdf/VendorResources/EDI_Documents/VendorFAQEDI.pdf): EDI is mandatory; any VAN or third-party EDI provider; no direct AS2 to Belk (AS2 only to the OpenText VAN); purchase orders batch nightly around 3am EST; 997 functional acknowledgments required; contacts EDI@Belk.com and ASN@Belk.com.
- [Belk Vendor Portal](https://vendorportal.belk.com/VendorPortal/): login-gated supplier application (no public documentation behind it).
- Marketplace and drop-ship order flow runs through Rithum (formerly CommerceHub) rather than a Belk-hosted API.

## What does not exist

- developer.belk.com is a dangling CNAME to a legacy Apigee Edge devportal edge (dev-belk.devportal.apigee.com -> ui-ea.apigee.net); it presents a *.apigee.com certificate and serves nothing owned by Belk.
- api.belk.com is an Akamai edge with a hostname-mismatched *.test.edgekey.net certificate answering 503; it is not a public API.
- No /.well-known/ document (security.txt, openid-configuration, oauth-authorization-server, api-catalog, ai-plugin.json, agent-card.json, apis.json) is served on any Belk host.
